# Conditional native development contract V3

Status: selected conditional implementation under the user's V3 exception (2026-10-11), not Claude-reviewed or biologically validated. Mode `vtc_development_v3`. All missing biological values below are explicit scenario hypotheses, not measurements. No coefficient fitting.

## Source mapping and scientific choices

Reference: K. Smolenova / WUR VTC (2022-2025), adapted from Jochem Evers FSPM_BASIC, local Downloads/vtc-main. Original source is preserved and hashed. Code license remains unresolved; local use only, no redistribution. Paper: https://doi.org/10.1093/insilicoplants/diaf022 (four non-Micro-Tom cultivars). Executable replacement mapping: `Apex.shouldProduceVegetative/Generative` and `rewrites.rgg` thermal threshold become a native dynamic apex state machine; `GrowingOrgan.calcSinkStrength/calcAssimilateAllocation` become normalized age-target demand and conservative proportional allocation; independent age-driven `calcDimensions` becomes mass-funded geometry. The original NRH function is copied verbatim into the active entrypoint. Original developmental routines are replaced, not falsely claimed to execute unmodified.

Carvalho 2011 https://doi.org/10.1186/1746-4811-7-18 informs the earlier fork contract's Micro-Tom priors. The two-successive-truss interpretation is a conditional program, not a verified universal rule. GroIMP polygon implementation follows https://wiki.grogra.de/doku.php?id=tutorials:leaf-triangulation . A2 definitions are normative; they do not validate any coefficient.

1. Daily chronological clock; thermal increment `max(0,(Tday*hours+Tnight*(24-hours))/24 - base_temperature_c)`. Germination threshold 206.3 degree-days and phyllochron 37.9 degree-days are borrowed Merlice values. Base 5.7 C is hypothetical. Birth decisions occur at end boundaries, after light/maintenance and before growth. First 9 compounds precede first truss (earlier 8-vegetative prior interpreted explicitly as first truss above leaf 9). Cadence is a user-assumed list of leaf counts between terminal trusses, initially `[2,1,0]`. Each truss terminates its own axis; one explicit sympodial successor is created until adjacent trusses with zero intervening leaves trigger `SP_STOP_RULE=two_successive_trusses`. No unrestricted side shoots or pruning. Existing tissues keep growing after the final stop.
2. Initiation clock proceeds independently of carbon. Newly initiated tissues start at zero mass and zero optical area. Expansion is allocation-funded; emergence requires positive funded mass and a separately recorded minimum age. Primordia accumulate demand but cannot intercept light before emergence. Geometry committed at boundary k is first used by light interval k. No same-interval pre-birth assimilation. Seed reserve is an explicit initial gC stock, used only once; seed begins without free photosynthetic blades.
3. Potential cumulative mass is a normalized logistic: `Mmax*(sigmoid(k*(age-mid))-sigmoid(-k*mid))/(1-sigmoid(-k*mid))`, clamped to [0,Mmax]. Daily demand is positive target-minus-actual mass times carbon_fraction/growth_efficiency. This allows delayed catch-up and never overwrites funded mass. Each tissue type (root, internode, petiole, rachis, leaflet, truss axis, flower) has its own Mmax, duration/midpoint and maintenance coefficient. Compound/axis/apex/attachment nodes are massless aggregates; their tissue children carry all mass. Root is an explicit physiological pool with its own target, no geometric root model.
4. Original fixed-coefficient Thornley NRH uses per-leaflet absorbed umol/s divided by represented one-sided polygon area. Gross CO2 -> gC by 12.011e-6; per-organ maintenance is `mass_gDM * maintenance_gC_gDM_day`, charged once for ALL tissues, including leaves and root. No separate leaf respiration term. Temperature affects development only, not NRH or maintenance; CO2 is stored but inactive. No claims of physiological temperature/CO2 response. Optical absorptance=1 is an explicitly hypothetical black-surface test law, not measured optics.
5. Within each plant: resource = previous reserve + gross assimilation - maintenance; funded allocation=min(nonnegative resource,sum demand), proportional to organ demand; structural increment=allocation*efficiency, growth respiration=allocation*(1-efficiency), DM increment=structural/carbon_fraction; surplus stays reserve. Negative resource is an explicit deficit; no mass catabolism or hidden subsidy; publication fails. Birth structural carbon is included in each organ's first positive allocation and separately linked in emergence events. All transfers appear once in organ flux and plant ledgers. No fruit set/maturation/yield, turnover or nutrient feedback.
6. Internode length=`cap * cbrt(mass/Mmax)` and width similarly, with a hypothetical 12 mm cap (NOT Merlice 72 mm). Leaflet area=`mass*SLA`, diamond outline length/width ratio=2, two triangles with area=L*W/2. Full outline convention retained; no automatic browned-area removal. Petiole/rachis/truss/flower dimensions scale by cbrt funded mass fraction; no mass-free geometry. Leaflet number indexed by true compound order (1-2: one pair, later: two; alternate three-pair scenarios allowed). Middle-pair weights explicit and normalized per compound; no intercalary area multiplier. One bend per actual lateral node, downward-positive; theta excludes terminal bend. Insertion and two azimuths exported separately; no phototropism. Blade curl and flat/bent thresholds remain on hold.
7. Seed and observation routes are distinct. Seed runs export every day including 0..30 and continue unchanged afterward; days_after_sowing is never output-day index. Observation importer preserves Stage-2 IDs and status/frame and requires explicit metric geometry, topology, true-order mapping, ages, masses, reserves and frame transform/rotation evidence; unknown values cannot become measured. A separate conditional supplement may supply missing hidden state with provenance. Fixtures remain fixtures. Replay means rerunning frozen inputs from original state; no dimension-only checkpoint resume.
