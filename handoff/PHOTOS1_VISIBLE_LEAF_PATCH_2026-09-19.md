# B1 visible-region partial surface

Implemented `tools/visible_leaf_patch.py` and `.html`, with `tools/tests/test_visible_leaf_patch.py` (5 tests passed). This standalone diagnostic tool consumes an existing capture, independently triangulated physical-point CSV and independently marked 2D regions. It parameterizes observed 3D landmarks by PCA, Delaunay-interpolates their 3D positions without flattening them, subdivides candidate triangles, and retains only rasterized projected footprints inside every available observed region (at least two solved source poses). It never pairs polygon indices or fills a missing physical landmark.

Current artifact: `build/photos1_manual3d_20260919/visible_B1_review11_final/index.html` (local URL on port 8825). Inputs are the current `edited_roundtrip06` capture, depth CSV and annotations. Five B1 landmarks support 768 subdivided candidate triangles; 107 remain after clipping to the two conservative visible regions. This is a small approximately 19.16 mm2 interpolated patch, not the whole leaflet or measured leaf area. Original b3 remains absent. The existing three partial surfaces and point-depth output were not replaced.

Photo overlays show projected interpolated faces in green, independently drawn image regions in magenta and actual observed landmarks in yellow. Actual detected board marker IDs and photo hashes accompany both images. All five observed point labels fit in the exported crop, including points outside the retained partial patch. A standalone PLY and JSON record are included. No source photos, calibration, core/config/docs or training data were changed. No learned model ran.

Validation: analytical synthetic concavity is not bridged; fewer than three finite landmarks or fewer than two region views cannot produce a surface; conflicting point positions and photo hashes fail; behind-camera and out-of-bounds geometry are excluded. Artifact integration test verifies source image changes fail before creating output. Actual B1 artifact counts, point identities, source-linked crop bounds and executable JS syntax checked; browser rendered surface and source-photo/marker captions inspected.
