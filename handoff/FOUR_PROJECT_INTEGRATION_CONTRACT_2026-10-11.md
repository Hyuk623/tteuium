A 43200 s numerical-fixture mode uses the same rate equations with elapsed-duration scaling, explicit per-interval illumination duration, degree-day increments and physical-day emergence wait. CSV boundary counters are indices; timestamps/duration in checkpoint metadata define time. Half-day fixtures are numerical sensitivity tests using averaged climate, not a validated subdaily biological response or a measured lamp clock. Other schedule/substep fields are rejected.

Optics: black actual native triangle meshes, absorbed flux and absorbed PPFD on one-sided area. Incident PPFD, scattering and nonzero tissue transmission are unavailable. Phone ratios remain separate relative metadata. Native leaf absorption is not a complete whole-scene photon budget. Fixed temperature/CO2 physiology and nutrient growth response remain disabled.

Each step binds native input/model/completion hashes, previous checkpoint, geometry/topology, placement, environment, lamp/optics, backend/runtime and stochastic settings. Re-collection is idempotent; replay is a fresh engine trajectory and verifies an existing prefix. A failed enabled nutrient ledger prevents publication; prescribed uptake is never represented as a growth constraint. Later observations are compared read-only at matched time/identity/area basis. No assimilation is implemented or implied.
